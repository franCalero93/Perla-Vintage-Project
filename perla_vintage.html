OUR STORY

Curated with care, worn with intention

Perla Vintage was born from a love of fashion that outlasts trends. Based in New York City, we source pieces from across Europe — flea markets in London, hidden shops in Spain, estate sales in Italy, and vintage fairs in France.

Every garment is hand-selected, cleaned, and photographed by us. We believe sustainable fashion isn't a sacrifice — it's a discovery.
