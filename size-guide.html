FIND YOUR REFERENCE

How to Measure

These are body measurements, not measurements taken from a garment laid flat.

Bust
Measure around the fullest part of your bust.
Waist
Measure around your natural waistline.
Hips
Measure around the fullest part of your hips.

Vintage sizing can differ significantly from modern sizing. For the best fit, always refer to the individual measurements listed on each product page.
